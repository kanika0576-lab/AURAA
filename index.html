How it works

One continuous journey. Five features.

01

Set the trip

Enter pickup and destination. AURA geocodes real places and turns on a silent Check-In for your expected arrival — no panic button required.

02

Choose with context

Fast, Balanced, or Resilient — each with a visible confidence meter and a human explanation computed from real data.

03

Travel with live awareness

Clock, ETA countdown, distance remaining, and a one-tap situation assistant. If plans change, AURA re-routes or finds a place to wait.

04

Check-In, silently

Check-In confirms safe arrival automatically. If you're late, a gentle nudge comes first — escalation to a trusted contact happens only as a last resort.

05

Arrive & confirm

Check-in confirms automatically. Trusted contact was never notified. Journey log stays on your device.

Principles

The ground rules under every feature.

🔐

Privacy-first, user-in-control

No data overreach. Location stays in the journey and is shared only when you choose. You always make the final call.

⚖️

No overclaiming safety

AURA never says a route is "safe." It communicates uncertainty honestly — the confidence meter exists because certainty can't be faked.

🔄

Real-time adaptability

A journey is a living plan. AURA re-routes from fresh data and tells you what it changed and why.
